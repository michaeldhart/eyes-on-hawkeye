As Mayor, | am writing to express my enthusiastic interest in your proposed development project (Project
Hawkeye), to be located in Belvidere generally along Genoa Road, south of Interstate 90 (Boone County).

City staff are committed to providing visionary leadership and guidance to ensure that growth and well-
being of our residents while fostering development and Innovation in the City of Belvidere continues as a
priority. | believe Project Hawkeye aligns with these goals and benefits our community significantly.

We believe that Project Hawkeye is poised to provide long-term economic benefits for the citizens of
Belvidere and the surrounding region. Creating high-skilled jobs, increasing tax revenue, and attracting
ancillary businesses will contribute to our local economy's diversification and growth.

As well, this proposed state-of-the-art data center development will continue to position the City of
Belvidere as a hub for technological advancement development projects with the potential to attract
further investment and talent to our Growth Corridor.

utility services and entitlements to the entire territory, not only the property under Datawatt Energy's
control, but also the surrounding territory. Of course, annexation and development are subject to City

Council review and approval.
