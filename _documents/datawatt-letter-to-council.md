Re: Project Hawkeye
Dear Mayor Morris and City Council of Belvidere:

Over the past two months, residents of Belvidere have come to City Hall to speak about Project Hawkeye.
We hear and respect the concerns of all residents.

We want you, the Council and the community to understand why we have not presented the project
publicly before now. Our most critical priority has been to establish whether ComEd and PJM can deliver
power to this location to support the project; without that answer there is no Project Hawkeye. At the
same time, we have been working through details that determine whether a project like Hawkeye would
be a benefit to the people of Belvidere rather than a burden on them: electricity rates, water use, noise,
financial commitments the project would make to the City and the schools, and the other matters residents

have raised.

Our intention has always been to bring the project to the City in the normal course — annexation, zoning
and a special use permit — with complete supporting materials that the Mayor, the Council and residents
could evaluate for themselves. To date, no applications have been filed with the City and no requests for
financial commitments have been or will be asked of the City.

We understand why learning about the project from news reports and records requests, rather than from
us, has caused concern.

What we can say now, in plain terms, is this. The campus would use a closed-loop cooling system that
minimizes water use. ComEd's tariffs and PJM's policies are designed to ensure that Project Hawkeye
carries the cost of powering and energizing its operations — not the residents of Belvidere. Noise limits
and setbacks from homes would be written into any agreement and enforceable by the City. The project
could become among the largest property taxpayers in Belvidere, with revenue going to support
Belvidere's schools, park district and public works, and to Boone County. Datawatt Energy will not ask

the City for tax incentives.

Residents of Belvidere should have the information to judge for themselves whether Project Hawkeye is
right for them and for the City. Datawatt Energy will hold an open public forum in Belvidere, to which
the Council and all residents are invited, where the project can be explained and questioned directly.
