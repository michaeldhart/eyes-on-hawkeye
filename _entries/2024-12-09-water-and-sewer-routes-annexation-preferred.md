summary: "Halligan emails the mayor and city staff ahead of a planned talk with “our neighbor,” attaching two city documents: a Genoa Road interchange water main loop and a southeast sanitary sewer extension. One is a 2022 city submission to the Region 1 Planning Council. He understands water will reach Fern Hill Rd. along the site's boundary without needing the neighbor's easement or annexation. Sewer, though, appears to run through the neighbor's land south of I-90, which would require an easement or annexation. City Attorney Mike Drella replies that annexation is preferred, if feasible, for both water and sewer, and that staff must verify the actual routes. Halligan says Datawatt will stress annexation with the neighbor."
