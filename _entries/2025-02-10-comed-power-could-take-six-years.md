summary: "David Halligan of Datawatt emails the mayor and City Attorney, copying JLL's Conor Mullady, with a status update, apologizing for not being in touch since late 2024. Datawatt has focused on connecting to ComEd. It says data center demand is outpacing ComEd's capacity and that ComEd is now suggesting delivery of power could take more than six years from a load request. That makes it hard to attract customers who need immediate power. Datawatt says it is working on ways to power the site independently of ComEd. Two days later, JLL and the mayor arrange a call for the following morning at 10 AM (pp. 206–207)."
