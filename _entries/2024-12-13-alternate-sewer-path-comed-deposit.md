It would not create the contiguity needed for annexation, but would give the site access to full city utilities. David Halligan of Datawatt replies that Datawatt is considering starting ComEd's load request process right away, which requires posting a $1 million deposit, so every sewer option is welcome. After a call that afternoon, Halligan sends the mayor and Drella contact details for Pepper Construction's energy team (p. 308)."
