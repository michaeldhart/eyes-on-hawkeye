Eyes on Hawkeye is a volunteer project by residents who want to understand what our local government is doing about a proposed data center, and who think everyone else should be able to understand it too.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Documents.** Public records the city has released, saved here as the city published them. Where we know the original web address, we link to it.
- **A timeline.** The documents and the events inside them, in order, so you can see what happened and when.
- **Search.** Full-text search across everything we've posted.

## How We Work

1. We get documents from the city's own public releases or from public records requests.
2. We keep an unedited copy of each file and link to where it came from.
3. Many of the files are scans. We run them through text recognition so they can be searched. That text is machine-made and can be wrong; the PDF is the record.
4. We write a short summary to help you find your way. Summaries are ours. The documents speak for themselves.
5. If we get something wrong, [tell us]({{ "/contact/" | relative_url }}) and we'll fix it and note the correction.

## What We Are Not

We're not the city and we don't speak for it. We don't have inside information. Everything here is something any resident could request, and we'd like to save you the trouble.
