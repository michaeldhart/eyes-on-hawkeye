Timeline

Scroll sideways through time. Select a dot to see what the record says.

Show or hide record types

Press Tab to reach the records, then use the arrow keys to move between them, Home and End to jump to the first or last, and Enter to select one. With the timeline itself focused, the arrow keys scroll sideways. The same records are available as a list above the timeline.

Select a dot on the timeline to preview that record.
